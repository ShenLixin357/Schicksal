Changes: selected stars through visual magnitude 9.5; omitted the Sun;
selected fields; normalized Cartesian position and velocity by distance;
converted to a compact float32 binary format; extracted names/designations.
Missing B-V values receive a neutral display colour only, not an observation.
The source digest and format are recorded in mafraer-hyg-v41.json.

The application, projection and interaction code are independently written.
