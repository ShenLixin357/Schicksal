## Real-star observatory

The observatory ships a compact derivative of the HYG v4.1 catalogue by David Nash / AstroNexus, containing **99,049 actual stars through visual magnitude 9.5**. It contains no invented stars or generated star-field bitmap. The data retains catalogue position, brightness, B-V colour index and supplied Cartesian space motion.

- Data: `public/data/mafraer-hyg-v41.bin` and `public/data/mafraer-hyg-v41.json`
- Credit/license: `public/data/MAFRAER-STAR-DATA-LICENSE.txt` (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- Reproducible conversion: `scripts/prepare-mafraer-stars.py`; source SHA-256 is in the metadata.
- Source: https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/blob/main/hyg/CURRENT/hygdata_v41.csv
- Catalogue schema and license: https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/blob/main/hyg/README.md

It starts with **Polaris (HIP 11767)** selected and tracked at the eyepiece centre. Users can drag, scroll/pinch, use keyboard arrows and +/−, select a star, track it, return to Polaris, change field of view (2°–100°), and jump to the Big Dipper, Cassiopeia, Vega or Sirius. Optional northern asterism lines and an equatorial grid use real star coordinates.

Observer presets include approximate city-centre Singapore, Jining and Beijing, plus manual coordinates and optional explicit browser geolocation. Coordinates are stored only as a local browser preference. Time follows the device clock, supports a ±12-hour scrubber and a manual date/time. Tracking keeps the chosen star centred while the rest of the sky responds to time/location changes. The geometric horizon masks stars that are below the selected observer's horizon; it may be disabled to inspect the atlas.

Calculation model: HYG J2000 vectors with supplied space motion, IAU 1976 precession, USNO mean sidereal time (UTC approximates UT1), geometric equatorial-to-horizontal transform, and gnomonic optical projection. Display brightness uses catalogue magnitude with a perceptual render scale. Unknown B-V receives a neutral display colour, as recorded in data metadata.

This is an **ideal dark-sky fixed-star simulation**, not live camera imagery or a weather/visibility forecast. It does not include planets, deep-sky imagery, atmospheric refraction, nutation or aberration. Dates are limited to 2000–2100. These limits are also stated briefly in the observatory UI.

Calculation references:
- https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/GAST
- https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/alt_az
- IAU 1976 rotation reference and independent numeric validation: https://github.com/liberfa/erfa/blob/master/src/pmat76.c and https://github.com/liberfa/erfa/blob/master/src/t_erfa_c.c
