## Locked animation
The exact approved animation code comes from the Schicksal branch demo/solstice-dendrite-back-back-reveal-20261119. During entrance, both rotating physical card faces show the original card BACK and Holy Light is composited OVER the card. Upon clicking, the second face reveals the real FRONT, which moves above the Holy Light. The approved V4 six-arm snow refraction and reveal are unchanged (1.42 seconds). Holy Light parameters: intensity 105%, width 100%, haze 100%; no glitter dots. Original 0.95s beam formation and 4.45s card descent are preserved.

## How to play
Open complete-animation.html directly in Safari/Chrome. The four original PNGs are built in, so no ZIP import is needed. Press "Guess What?" to start; click while descending to skip to hover; click again to reveal. PNG originals are also in this ZIP for use in Procreate and the Schicksal website.

## Design lock
No redrawing, resizing, recoloring or replacing of the user's approved PNG art during bundling. Preserve PNG bytes, transparency, sharp typography, all framing, brightness, and the V4 shader. The original animation branch and website main are not overwritten.
